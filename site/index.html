Every bet on the board goes through the same pipeline:

  1. Team ratings. Each team gets an offense and a defense rating, fit on every game over the last season or so. Recent games count more, and opponents' strength is built in.
  2. Game adjustments. Home field, rest and back-to-backs. In MLB, starting pitchers (FIP, recent starts) and park factors. For NFL and NHL, key injuries like a starting QB or goalie.
  3. Score simulation. Ratings become a full distribution of possible scores, built for each sport, which prices every moneyline, spread and total.
  4. Market check. DraftKings prices get the vig removed and are blended with our model. A pick shows an edge only when the data genuinely disagrees with the market.
  5. Calibration. A walk-forward backtest tunes each sport so a "60%" pick hits about 60% of the time.

Grades: A clearly +EV and more likely than not. B +EV. C close to a fair price. D the price is too steep.

No model hits 100%. Parlays multiply the book's margin, and a 3-leg parlay of 70% legs still loses about two times in three. Bet only what you can afford to lose. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
